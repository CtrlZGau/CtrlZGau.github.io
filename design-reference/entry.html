Notes on action chunking

[Sample entry — replace with your own.] Instead of predicting one action per timestep, a chunked policy predicts the next k actions at once and executes them before querying again.

Fewer decisions per episode means fewer chances for small errors to compound — which is exactly where single-step imitation learning tends to fall apart.

actions = policy(obs)[:k]   # k-step chunk
for a in actions:
    robot.step(a)
