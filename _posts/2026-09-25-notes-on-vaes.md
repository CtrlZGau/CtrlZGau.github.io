Notes on variational autoencoders: why a plain autoencoder's latent space can't
generate, what the KL term is actually constraining, and the derivation that turns
that constraint into the reconstruction-plus-divergence loss. Then the conditional
variant, the reparameterisation trick, and VQ-VAE's codebook and commitment losses.
